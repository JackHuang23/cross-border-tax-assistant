Legal Basis & Citations

Cross-Border Individual Tax Assistant — the statutory basis and citation sources for every rule implemented in the engine. Methodology: Primary sources = government / official documents (State Taxation Administration 国家税务总局, State Council 国务院, Ministry of Finance / State Taxation Administration 财政部/税务总局, IRS, and the two governments). Secondary sources = non-government (PwC, KPMG, law firms) — used only for cross-checking figures. Language: This document is written in English; Chinese legal terms are kept in Chinese (e.g., 《个人所得税法》, 财税〔2015〕101 号, 居民个人).

I. China Tax Law (Primary · Government)

1. Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China

《中华人民共和国个人所得税法》（2018 amendment）

Source: State Taxation Administration (国家税务总局) URL: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html

Article 1 — 居民个人 / 非居民个人 (resident vs non-resident individual)

  • Citation: 《个人所得税法》Art. 1
  • Rule: An individual who is domiciled in China (在中国境内有住所), or who is not domiciled but has resided in China for 183 days or more in a tax year, is a resident individual (居民个人) and is taxed on worldwide income. Otherwise the individual is a non-resident individual (非居民个人), taxed only on China-source income.
  • Engine use: China residency determination (residency.py)

Article 2 — Categories of taxable income

  • Citation: 《个人所得税法》Art. 2
  • Rule: Nine categories of income are taxable, including (1) wages and salaries (工资、薪金所得), (2) remuneration for labour services (劳务报酬所得), (3) author's remuneration (稿酬所得), (4) royalties (特许权使用费所得), (5) business income (经营所得), (6) interest, dividends and bonuses (利息、股息、红利所得), (7) property lease income, (8) property transfer income, and (9) incidental income. For resident individuals, items (1)–(4) are consolidated as comprehensive income (综合所得).
  • Engine use: income classification

Article 3 — Tax rates

  • Citation: 《个人所得税法》Art. 3
  • Rule: Comprehensive income (综合所得) is taxed at progressive rates of 3%–45%; business income (经营所得) at 5%–35%; interest/dividends/property-transfer/incidental income at a flat 20%.
  • Engine use: tax-rate tables (constants.py)

Article 6 — Computation of taxable income (deductions)

  • Citation: 《个人所得税法》Art. 6
  • Rule: For resident individuals, comprehensive income taxable base = annual income minus RMB 60,000 (基本减除费用), special deductions (专项扣除) and special additional deductions (专项附加扣除) and other deductions determined by law (依法确定的其他扣除). For non-residents, monthly salary income is reduced by RMB 5,000. Labour remuneration, author's remuneration and royalties are reduced by 20% to arrive at the income amount; author's remuneration is then further reduced by 70% (i.e., ×80%×70% = ×56%).
  • Special deductions (专项扣除) = social insurance contributions (basic pension 基本养老保险, basic medical 基本医疗保险, unemployment 失业保险) and housing fund (住房公积金).
  • Special additional deductions (专项附加扣除) = children's education (子女教育), continuing education (继续教育), serious-illness medical expenses (大病医疗), housing loan interest (住房贷款利息), housing rent (住房租金), and support for the elderly (赡养老人).
  • Engine use: comprehensive-income taxable base (calculator.py)

Article 7 — Foreign tax credit

  • Citation: 《个人所得税法》Art. 7
  • Rule: A resident individual may credit foreign income tax paid against Chinese tax on foreign-source income, capped at the Chinese tax computed on that income.
  • Engine use: foreign tax credit (foreign_tax_credit_limit)

Article 11 & 13 — Filing deadlines

  • Citation: 《个人所得税法》Art. 11, Art. 13
  • Rule: Annual settlement (汇算清缴) for comprehensive income and reporting of foreign-source income must be completed between 1 March and 30 June of the following year.

2. Implementing Regulations of the Individual Income Tax Law

《中华人民共和国个人所得税法实施条例》（State Council Decree No. 707, effective 1 Jan 2019）

Source: State Taxation Administration (国家税务总局) URL: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3963364/content.html

Article 2 — Definition of "domicile"

  • Citation: 《实施条例》Art. 2
  • Rule: "Domiciled in China (在中国境内有住所)" means habitual residence in China by reason of household registration, family or economic interests (因户籍、家庭、经济利益关系而习惯性居住).
  • Engine use: domicile test (UI guidance)

Article 4 — Six-year rule and the 30-day reset

  • Citation: 《实施条例》Art. 4
  • Rule: A non-domiciled individual whose residence meets 183 days for less than six consecutive years is exempt from Chinese tax on foreign-source income paid by a foreign payer. Once 183 days has been met for six consecutive years, worldwide income becomes taxable. A single departure exceeding 30 days in any year resets the consecutive-year count.
  • Engine use: 6-year rule + 30-day reset (six_year_rule_triggered)

Article 5 — The 90-day rule

  • Citation: 《实施条例》Art. 5
  • Rule: A non-domiciled individual residing in China for no more than 90 days is exempt on China-source income paid by a foreign employer and not borne by that employer's Chinese establishment.
  • Engine use: 90-day rule (ninety_day_exemption)

Article 21 — Foreign tax credit limit

  • Citation: 《实施条例》Art. 21
  • Rule: Defines the credit limit (抵免限额) for foreign tax paid on foreign-source income, computed separately by country and by income category.
  • Engine use: foreign tax credit limit

3. Announcement No. 34 of 2019 (residence-day counting)

财政部 税务总局公告 2019 年第 34 号

Source: State Taxation Administration policy database URL: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c4151944/content.html

  • Citation: 财政部 税务总局公告 2019 年第 34 号
  • Rule: (1) A non-domiciled individual who meets 183 days in a tax year is taxable on worldwide income only if, in each of the previous six years (此前六年), the individual met 183 days and had no single departure exceeding 30 days; otherwise foreign-source income paid by a foreign payer is exempt. (2) Days of residence are counted by days of physical stay of a full 24 hours — a day of less than 24 hours is not counted (满 24 小时计入，不足 24 小时不计入).
  • Engine use: residence-day counting (full-24-hour rule) + precise six-year rule

4. Caishui [2015] No. 101 (A-share dividend differential)

财税〔2015〕101 号

  • Citation: 财税〔2015〕101 号
  • Rule: Dividends on A-shares held more than 1 year are exempt; held 1 month or less (含 1 个月) are taxed at an effective 20%; held more than 1 month up to 1 year (含 1 年) at an effective 10%.
  • Engine use: A-share dividend differential (≤1m 20% / 1m–1y 10% / >1y 0%)

5. Caishuizi [1998] No. 61 (A-share transfer exemption)

财税字〔1998〕61 号

  • Citation: 财税字〔1998〕61 号
  • Rule: Gains from transferring listed-company shares acquired on the public markets of the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges are exempt from individual income tax.
  • Engine use: A-share capital-gain exemption

II. U.S. Tax Law (Primary · Government)

6. Agreement Between the U.S. and China for the Avoidance of Double Taxation (1984)

Source: IRS (official treaty PDF) URL: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf

Article 4 — Residence (dual residency)

  • Citation: (U.S.–China Tax Treaty, art. 4, ¶ 2)
  • Original text:

    Where by reason of the provisions of paragraph 1 an individual is a resident of both Contracting States, then the competent authorities of the Contracting States shall determine through consultations the Contracting State of which that individual shall be deemed to be a resident for the purposes of this Agreement.

  • Engine use: dual-resident treatment (mutual agreement procedure, not a mechanical tie-breaker)

Article 9 — Dividends (10%)

  • Citation: (U.S.–China Tax Treaty, art. 9, ¶ 2)
  • Original text:

    ...if the recipient is the beneficial owner of the dividends the tax so charged shall not exceed 10 percent of the gross amount of the dividends.

  • Engine use: U.S. dividend withholding (NRA 30% → treaty 10%)

Article 11 — Royalties (10%) and the permanent-establishment exception

  • Citation: (U.S.–China Tax Treaty, art. 11, ¶¶ 2 & 4)
  • Original text (¶ 2):

    ...such royalties may also be taxed in the Contracting State in which they arise ... but if the recipient is the beneficial owner of the royalties, the tax so charged shall not exceed 10 percent of the gross amount of the royalties.

  • Original text (¶ 4, the "connection" test):

    The provisions of paragraphs 1 and 2 shall not apply if the beneficial owner of the royalties ... carries on business in the other Contracting State ... through a permanent establishment situated therein, or performs ... independent personal services from a fixed base situated therein, and the right or property ... is effectively connected with such permanent establishment or fixed base. In such case the provisions of Article 7 or 13, as the case may be, shall apply.

  • Engine use: cross-border royalties — passive royalties 10%; royalties effectively connected with a U.S. PE/fixed base taxed as business profits instead

Article 13 — Independent Personal Services (cross-border self-employment / contracting)

  • Citation: (U.S.–China Tax Treaty, art. 13, ¶ 1)
  • Original text:

    Income derived by an individual who is a resident of a Contracting State in respect of professional services or other activities of an independent character shall be taxable only in that Contracting State, unless he has a fixed base regularly available to him in the other Contracting State ... or he is present in that other Contracting State for a period or periods exceeding in the aggregate 183 days in the calendar year concerned.

  • Engine use: cross-border self-employment — taxable only in the residence state unless there is a U.S. fixed base or >183-day U.S. presence

Article 14 — Dependent Personal Services (cross-border employment)

  • Citation: (U.S.–China Tax Treaty, art. 14)
  • Original text:

    ...salaries, wages and other similar remuneration derived by a resident of a Contracting State in respect of an employment shall be taxable only in that Contracting State unless the employment is exercised in the other Contracting State ... remuneration ... shall be taxable only in the first-mentioned State if: (a) the recipient is present in the other Contracting State ... not exceeding in the aggregate 183 days; and (b) the remuneration is paid by ... an employer who is not a resident of the other Contracting State; and (c) the remuneration is not borne by a permanent establishment or a fixed base which the employer has in the other Contracting State.

  • Engine use: cross-border employment — the 183-day / non-resident-employer / no-PE triple test

Article 22 — Elimination of Double Taxation

  • Citation: (U.S.–China Tax Treaty, art. 22, ¶ 1)
  • Original text:

    In the People's Republic of China, double taxation shall be eliminated as follows: ... the amount of the United States income tax payable ... shall be allowed as a credit against the Chinese tax imposed on that resident.

  • Engine use: foreign tax credit

7. IRS Substantial Presence Test

Source: IRS URL: https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/substantial-presence-test

  • Citation: (IRS, Substantial Presence Test)
  • Original text:

    You are treated as present in the U.S. on any day you are physically present in the country, at any time during the day. However, there are exceptions to this rule. Do not count the following as days of presence ... Days you are in the U.S. for less than 24 hours, when you are in transit between two places outside the United States.

  • Engine use: U.S. residency determination and day-counting (any-time rule, five exclusions; "in transit <24h" is a combined AND condition)

8. IRS Publication 519, U.S. Tax Guide for Aliens

Source: IRS URL: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519

  • Citation: (IRS Pub. 519)
  • Rule — two classes of NRA income:
  • Effectively Connected Income (ECI) — income effectively connected with a U.S. trade or business, taxed at graduated (ordinary) rates rather than 30% withholding.
  • FDAP income (fixed, determinable, annual or periodic) — such as dividends, interest and royalties, subject to 30% withholding (or a lower treaty rate), unless effectively connected.
  • Rule — exempt individuals (days not counted for the SPT): foreign government-related individuals on A/G visas (diplomats), teachers/trainees on J/Q visas, and students on F/J/M/Q visas — their days of presence are excluded from the substantial presence test.
  • Engine use: NRA income classification; exempt-individual days (the exempt exclusion in us_countable_days)

Appendix: Citation Methodology

  1. Primary vs secondary: primary = government/official documents (State Taxation Administration 国家税务总局, State Council 国务院, Ministry of Finance / State Taxation Administration 财政部/税务总局, IRS, and the two governments); secondary = non-government institutions (PwC, KPMG, law firms).
  2. Language: Chinese legal terms are kept in Chinese; all other text is in English.
  3. In-text citation format: statutes use 《法名》Art. X or (Source, art. X); administrative documents use their document number (e.g., 财税〔2015〕101 号).
  4. Every computed figure in the engine carries its corresponding citation (see the cite / citations fields in tax_engine/ and app.py), so every number is traceable to its legal basis.
